# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical direction and help the team deliver a feasible, maintainable solution.

### Responsibilities
- Make or coordinate architecture and implementation decisions
- Surface technical estimates, dependencies, and risks
- Support code quality and consistency across the development team
- Identify when technical trade-offs affect scope or delivery

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Guide Developers through implementation and review
- Partner with QA/Testing and Security/Privacy Leads to incorporate quality and security needs early

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and decision records
- Engineering planning and code reviews
- Risk and dependency updates with the Project Manager

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX or Product Designers translate user needs into clear, usable, and accessible product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and identify usability needs
- Create user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Include accessibility and usability considerations in designs
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to connect user needs and product outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads to validate feasibility
- Partner with QA/Testing to make usability and accessibility expectations testable
- Keep the Project Manager informed when design decisions affect scope or milestones

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- User flows, prototypes, and design specifications
- Usability and accessibility feedback

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads coordinate the approach to verifying that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage and validation activities for the project
- Confirm acceptance criteria are testable and clarify gaps
- Coordinate defect triage and communicate quality risks
- Support release readiness with appropriate test evidence

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads on testability and defect resolution
- Align with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on quality risks and readiness
- Partner with Release / Operations Leads on smoke tests and deployment verification

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, results, and defect reports
- Acceptance-criteria reviews
- Quality and readiness updates

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance needs throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and risks
- Advise on safeguards, data handling, and required reviews
- Coordinate security or privacy assessments and track findings to resolution
- Escalate material risks through the agreed project process

### Collaboration
- Work with Engineering / Technical Leads and Developers to incorporate safeguards into the design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on validating security and privacy requirements
- Keep the Project Manager informed about risks, dependencies, and review timing
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders when requirements affect scope or customer commitments

### Typical Communication
- Risk and review findings
- Security and privacy requirements
- Remediation and escalation updates

---

## Release / Operations (SRE) Leads

### Role Summary
Release or Operations Leads prepare services and teams for safe deployment and reliable operation.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, operational handoff, and release procedures
- Define monitoring, alerting, and post-deployment verification needs
- Prepare rollback or mitigation plans with the delivery team
- Surface operational risks and support incident readiness

### Collaboration
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and readiness
- Work with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads on operability and observability
- Partner with QA/Testing on smoke tests and post-deployment verification
- Align with Customer Support / Success Representatives on support readiness and incident communications

### Typical Communication
- Deployment and rollback plans
- Readiness reviews and operational handoff notes
- Monitoring and post-deployment status

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Success Representatives bring customer-impact and supportability needs into delivery and release planning.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer feedback and support trends
- Identify support, training, and documentation needs for changes
- Prepare customer-facing teams for releases and known issues
- Relay post-release feedback and emerging customer-impacting problems

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to priorities and outcomes
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on stakeholder updates and customer communications
- Partner with Release / Operations Leads on support readiness and incident communications
- Provide Developers and QA/Testing with reproducible customer-impact details when issues arise

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and support trends
- Release briefings and support materials
- Escalations and post-release feedback

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads help teams define and instrument measures that show whether a project achieves its intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success criteria into measurable indicators and reporting needs
- Advise on data collection, instrumentation, and data quality
- Coordinate analysis of project outcomes and communicate limitations
- Support evidence-based decisions while respecting applicable privacy requirements

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to define outcome metrics and interpret results
- Coordinate with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads on instrumentation
- Align with the Project Manager on reporting cadence and milestone measurement
- Share findings with Stakeholders and consult Security / Privacy Leads when measurement involves sensitive data

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and instrumentation requirements
- Dashboards and outcome reports
- Data-quality notes and analysis findings

---

These roles are adaptable: depending on project size and skills, one person may cover multiple roles, or a role may be consulted only when needed. Make ownership and handoffs explicit for each project.
